OSHA Establishment Search ↗

Field guide

Manual Search
Searches OSHA by establishment name across the last five calendar years. Partial names work.
Underwriter Notes
Record your notes on any Serious, Willful, Repeat, and FAT/CAT's. Detail information from agent's or loss control.
Five Year History
Citation counts and penalty totals across all inspections shown. Deleted inspections are excluded.
Open / closed counts
An inspection with no close date is still open.
Show empty fields
Fields OSHA returned blank are hidden. Tick this to see them all, for manual entry.
Inspection row
Activity number, dates, state, citation counts and establishment name. Click the arrow to open the full record; click a column heading to sort.
FAT/CAT
Fatality or catastrophe inspection. Citations tied to the accident sort to the top and carry the same marker.
Addresses, NAICS, Advanced Notice
Site and mailing address as filed, the industry code, and whether notice was given before the visit.
Inspection Type / Scope / Safety-Health
Why the inspection happened, how much of the site it covered, and whether it was a safety or health inspection.
Employees / Owner Type / Migrant Labor
Headcount at the site, private or government employer, and whether migrant labor was involved.
Planning Guides
Which OSHA planning guides applied, by safety or health and by industry.
Violation Items
One block per citation, ranked Willful, Repeat, Serious, Other. Current and initial penalties roll up into Five Year History.
Initial vs Current Citation Type
What the citation was first issued as, and what it stands at now after any settlement or appeal.
Standard Cited
The OSHA standard behind the citation; the line underneath carries its text.
Issuance / Abatement Due / Contest Date
When it was issued, when the hazard had to be fixed, and the date the employer contested it, if they did.
Current / Initial Penalty
The penalty as it stands and as first proposed. A large gap usually means a settlement.
Latest Event
The most recent action on the citation, such as an informal or formal settlement.
Abatement Complete / FTA Penalty
Whether the hazard was fixed and how it was resolved if not. FTA = Penalty for Failure to Abate.
Final Order Date / Prompted By
When the citation became final, and what triggered it: accident, complaint, referral or imminent danger.
Hazard Category / Hazardous Substances / Emphasis Program
The hazard involved, any substance codes recorded, and whether it fell under an emphasis program.
Related Activity
Other complaints, referrals, accidents or inspections linked to this one, flagged as safety or health.
Investigation Summary
Appears for accident inspections: the incident narrative, event date and time, and the injured worker's details.
Degree / Nature of Injury, Occupation
How severe the injury was, what kind it was, and the worker's job. Some arrive as OSHA numeric codes.
Injury Detail
Part of body, source, event type, human and environmental factors, fall distances and any substance involved.
Keywords
OSHA's own keywords for the incident.
Deleted Inspections
Removed inspections, with the reason given. They can be restored.
